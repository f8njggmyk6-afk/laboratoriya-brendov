## Customer-facing discount policy

- Never advertise "5% below the whole market", "market median", or fictional previous prices.
- Each new complex project receives 5% off our stated base development estimate.
- Starting prices are computed as round(baseAmount * 95 / 100), once, in the catalog.
- Show base estimate, actual discounted starting price and a "5% included" label.
- Apply the same 5% to the agreed development-work estimate in the proposal, showing
  the base, discount and final amount before work starts.
- Hosting, domains, licenses, model/API consumption, payment fees and ongoing support
  are separate and excluded from the discount.
- Final scope, timeline, milestones and price require agreement after discovery.
- Do not change existing small-service prices or add quoted work to fixed-price totals.
